# Metric

> Metric is an AI-powered financial intelligence and accounting platform built for business owners, founders, and CEOs. It replaces spreadsheets and expensive CFO teams with a real-time financial dashboard and Max — an AI co-pilot (CF-AI) that answers financial questions instantly in plain language.

Metric is headquartered in Abu Dhabi (UAE) with offices in Rawalpindi (Pakistan), Singapore, and Riyadh (Saudi Arabia). It serves 200K+ businesses across 190+ countries and has processed over $4.2 billion in transactions. Plans start at AED 0/month (free tier available).

## Product

### Max AI (CF-AI — Chief Financial AI)
Max is Metric's embedded AI co-pilot. Users ask Max questions in plain language — "What's my runway?", "Can I afford a new hire?", "Where are my biggest expenses?" — and receive instant, context-aware answers drawn from their live financial data. Max also benchmarks performance against 200K+ businesses globally to surface industry insights.

### Accounting & Bookkeeping
- Transaction management via manual entry, CSV import, or integrations
- Payables and receivables tracking with partial payment support
- Multiple account management (e.g., founder vs. business accounts)
- Real-time financial dashboard with trend graphs

### Reports & Invoicing
- 8 auto-generated report types (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and more)
- Customisable invoices with bank details and instant payment options
- Customer, product, and vendor tracking

### Compliance & VAT
- UAE VAT calculation and reporting
- Audit-ready exports
- Year-end reporting support
- Tax advisory (add-on on Pro/Enterprise)

### Integrations
QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Xero, Shopify, direct bank feeds. Salla and Zid coming soon.

## Who It's For

- **Business owners and founders** who need financial clarity without a full accounting team
- **CEOs and operators** who make hiring, spending, and investment decisions and need instant answers
- **Startups from day one** — free tier allows early-stage companies to start structured bookkeeping immediately
- **UAE and GCC businesses** — VAT compliance and AED-first pricing make it well-suited for the region, though the platform serves 190+ countries
